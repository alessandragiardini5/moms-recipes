Toasted Pumpkin Seeds

From the kitchen of Mom
Oven250–300°F
ToastAbout 30 min
Mom's original card · tap to enlarge

Ingredients

Method tap a step to mark it done

  1. Wash the seeds and separate them from the pulp.
  2. Spread the seeds thinly on a baking sheet with the butter and salt (and garlic powder, if using).
  3. Toast in a low oven, 250–300°F, for about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until crisp and brown.
