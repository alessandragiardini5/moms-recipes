Pollo alla Milanese

Breaded Chicken Cutlets
From the kitchen of Mom
CookPan-fried
Serves1 breast per person
Mom's original card · tap to enlarge

Ingredients

Method tap a step to mark it done

  1. Butterfly the chicken breasts (open them up) and pound them thin.
  2. Put the egg and salt in a bowl and whip.
  3. Dip the breasts in the egg.
  4. Pour bread crumbs onto a plate and press both sides of each breast into them.
  5. Pour vegetable oil into a pan and fry the breasts. Turn when golden.
  6. When golden on both sides, lay them on paper towels.
