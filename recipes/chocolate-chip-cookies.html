Chocolate Chip Cookies

From the kitchen of Angela
Oven375°F
Bake9–11 min

Ingredients

Method tap a step to mark it done

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the flour, baking soda and salt.
  3. In a large mixer bowl, beat the butter, both sugars and the vanilla until creamy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each.
  5. Gradually beat in the flour mixture, then stir in the chocolate morsels and nuts.
  6. Drop by rounded tablespoons onto ungreased baking sheets.
  7. Bake 9–11 minutes, until golden brown (start checking at 9).
  8. Let stand on the sheets for 2 minutes, then move to wire racks to cool completely.
