Potato Salad

Method tap a step to mark it done

  1. Boil the potatoes until soft. Hard-boil the eggs.
  2. Let the potatoes cool slightly, until you can handle them, then peel.
  3. Cut the potatoes into bite-size pieces.
  4. Peel and chop the eggs.
  5. Mix the mayonnaise, cider vinegar and dry mustard.
  6. Combine the potatoes and eggs with salt and pepper, then add the dressing until it tastes right.
